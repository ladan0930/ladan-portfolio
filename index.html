Injection-Molded Snap-Fit Shim for Aerodynamic Skirt Assemblies : Internship project

SolidWorks · Injection Molding · DFM/DFA

Context
  • Aerodynamic skirt assemblies use shims to maintain alignment and transfer loads through the bracket mounting system.
Objective
  • Redesigned an aluminum shim into a snap-fit, injection-molded nylon component compatible with existing skirt hardware.
  • Met load requirements, long-term durability targets, and manufacturability constraints without complex tooling.
Design, Prototyping & Testing
  • Analytical Validation: Performed hand calculations to size undercuts and estimate insertion forces, ensuring strain remained within the elastic limits of the specified Nylon 6/6.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM): Optimized geometry with draft angles, uniform wall thickness, and controlled undercuts to minimize tooling complexity and reduce cycle-time variance.
  • Modular Tooling: Engineered interchangeable mold inserts to accommodate multiple design variants, significantly reducing capital expenditure on tooling.
  • Prototyping & Verification: Validated assembly kinematics and interference fits using high-fidelity 3D-printed prototypes prior to final tool release.
Results
  • Reduced assembly time from 20 minutes to under 5.
  • Lowered material costs by over $12 000, reducing weight by 35%, and simplifying supplier logistics.

Stirling Engine Mechanical Design

Engine · SolidWorks · 3D-Model

Project Background
  • Objective: To design and model a functional Alpha-type Stirling engine, focusing on precise linkage kinematics and thermal-to-mechanical energy conversion.
Key Technical Features
  • Virtual Prototyping: Developed a high-fidelity 3D model of a 3-roller peristaltic pump in SolidWorks, focusing on the kinematic relationship between the rotor and the occlusion bed.
  • DFM & DFA Optimization: Applied Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles to the pump housing to ensure 3D-printability and utilized Design for Assembly (DFA) to standardize M-series fasteners, reducing part count and assembly time.
  • Mechanical Clearance Validation: Conducted static interference checks to verify the precise squeeze (occlusion) on the tubing, ensuring a theoretical seal without over-stressing the motor.
