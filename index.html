Injection-Molded Snap-Fit Shim for Aerodynamic Skirt Assemblies : Internship project

SolidWorks · Injection Molding · DFM/DFA

Context
  • Aerodynamic skirt assemblies use shims to maintain alignment and transfer loads through the bracket mounting system.
Objective
  • Redesigned an aluminum shim into a snap-fit, injection-molded nylon component compatible with existing skirt hardware.
  • Met load requirements, long-term durability targets, and manufacturability constraints without complex tooling.
Design, Prototyping & Testing
  • Performed hand calculations to size the undercut and estimate insertion force; checked allowable strain to keep the specified nylon within elastic limits during assembly.
  • Applied DFM constraints (draft, uniform wall thickness, and controlled undercuts) to reduce tooling complexity and improve cycle-time reliability.
  • Designed interchangeable mold inserts to vary feature thickness across variants without requiring a new tool.
  • Validated fit and assembly behavior with a 3D-printed prototype before release.
Injection molding Snap-fit design DFM/DFA Prototyping

Stirling Engine — Mechanical Design

Engine · SolidWorks · 3D-Model

Project Background
  • Objective: To design and model a functional Alpha-type Stirling engine, focusing on precise linkage kinematics and thermal-to-mechanical energy conversion.
Key Technical Features
  • Kinematics: Modeled a 90-degree phase shift between the power piston and displacer to optimize the thermodynamic cycle.
  • Design for Assembly (DFA): Utilized standard fastener sizes (M3/M4) and incorporated press-fit bearings for the crankshaft to ensure smooth rotation.
  • Material Selection: Specified high-thermal-conductivity materials for the hot end and transparent borosilicate glass for the displacer cylinder to allow for visual inspection of the stroke.
